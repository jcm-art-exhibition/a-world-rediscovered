Passages

Step through, room by room, into the world the Bible actually came from

Passages is a museum built from rooms, not chapters — each door opening onto another piece of the biblical world's ancient setting: its stories, symbols, languages, cultures, and neighboring literature. Scripture and ancient Near Eastern texts are brought into conversation, not to erase their differences or prescribe a conclusion, but to reveal the world that actually surrounded them.

You are invited to look closely, engage with evidence from the cultures surrounding the biblical texts, and consider a more expansive understanding of the world their authors inhabited. Every interpretation presented here is connected to named scholars and traceable sources, allowing curiosity—not certainty—to lead the way.
