Substrata

An immersive journey beneath the surface of the biblical world

Substrata explores some of the earliest foundations of the biblical world. Each room descends into another layer of the Bible's ancient setting—its stories, symbols, languages, cultures, and neighboring literature. Scripture and ancient Near Eastern texts are brought into conversation, not to erase their differences or prescribe a conclusion, but to reveal the rich historical ground beneath them.

You are invited to look closely, engage with evidence from the cultures surrounding the biblical texts, and consider a more expansive understanding of the world their authors inhabited. Every interpretation presented here is connected to named scholars and traceable sources, allowing curiosity—not certainty—to lead the way.
