The Passage

A World Rediscovered is built in layers, not chapters — each room a stratum uncovered, opening onto another piece of the ancient setting behind Scripture: its stories, symbols, languages, cultures, and neighboring literature. Scripture and Ancient Near Eastern texts are brought into conversation, not to erase their differences or prescribe a conclusion, but to reveal the world that actually surrounded them.

You are invited to look closely, engage with evidence from the cultures surrounding the biblical texts, and consider a more expansive understanding of the world their authors inhabited. Every interpretation presented here is connected to named scholars and traceable sources, allowing curiosity—not certainty—to lead the way.
