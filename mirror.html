The Mirror Exhibit

No One Reads Without a Lens

Every reader brings assumptions to a text without realizing it. Our expectations, traditions, and frames of reference shape what we notice, what we connect, and what we may overlook.

The Mirror is designed to make some of those interpretive habits visible. You’ll respond to a series of questions, see the lenses your responses reveal, and then revisit biblical passages through other lenses to notice what changes.

There are no correct answers. Choose the response that most closely reflects how you would naturally understand each passage. Don’t try to choose the answer you think you should give. The invitation is to notice the habits that shape how you read.
