No One Reads Without a Lens

Every reader brings a worldview to the text before reading a single word. Our habits, expectations, traditions, and frames of reference shape what stands out and what passes unnoticed.

This exhibit does not test what you know about the Bible. It helps you notice how you read it, including the interpretive habits and filters you bring to every passage, whether or not you have ever named them.
