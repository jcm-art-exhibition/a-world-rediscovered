The Map Exhibit

A world rediscovered, one excavation at a time.

For centuries, traces of the world surrounding the Bible lay buried, scattered, or unread. Archaeological discoveries gradually brought its cities, languages, texts, and cultures back into view.

Explore where they were found, when they entered modern scholarship, and what they changed about our understanding of the biblical world.
