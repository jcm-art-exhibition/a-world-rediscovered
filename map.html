The Discovery Map

A world rediscovered, one excavation at a time.

For centuries, much of the world surrounding the Bible was known primarily through the biblical texts themselves. Archaeological discoveries across Mesopotamia, Egypt, Canaan, and the Levant gradually brought that world back into view.

Explore where these discoveries were made, when they entered modern scholarship, and how they changed the way the Bible was read.
