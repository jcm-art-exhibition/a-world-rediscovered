A Note Before You Look

Enūma Eliš and Genesis 1 come from the same broad world and window of history — Mesopotamia and the Levant, in the centuries before and after 600 BCE. Scholars in Ancient Near Eastern studies, including John Walton and Michael Heiser, have long noted that Babylonian and Israelite creation accounts share a common shape: the waters, the split, the lights, humanity, and rest — the same five beats, in the same order. What follows sets two of those accounts side by side, one stratum at a time, so you can see that shape for yourself.
